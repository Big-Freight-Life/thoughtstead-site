# Costs

One price, and it covers everything you need to run Thoughtstead.

| Item | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Thoughtstead (hosted) | **$20/month** |
| Hosting | Included |
| AI usage — embeddings, enrichment, search | Included, at no markup |
| Exporting your data | Free, any time |

There is no API key to obtain, no cloud account to create, and no per-token
bill to watch. If you cancel, you can still export everything first.

## Self-hosted

Self-hosting — running Thoughtstead entirely on your own infrastructure, with
your own database and your own AI keys — ships after the hosted apps. Its
pricing will be announced at launch. The cost shape will be different: you pay
for your own hosting and AI usage directly, at whatever those providers charge.

If you want it, [email us](mailto:support@bfl.design?subject=Self-hosted%20waitlist)
and we will tell you when it is ready.

## What happens if something degrades

Thoughtstead never loses a capture. Every thought saves immediately, raw, before
any AI touches it. If enrichment or semantic search is temporarily degraded, the
app says so plainly on every AI surface, and the backlog processes automatically
once service is restored.
