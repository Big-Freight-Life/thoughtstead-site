# Costs

The whole pitch of Thoughtstead is that you own it — which means the costs
are honest and itemized, not folded into a subscription. Here's the full
picture.

| Item | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Thoughtstead | $299 once (launch price **$199** with code `LAUNCH`) |
| Vercel | $0 (Hobby tier covers hosting) |
| Supabase | $0 (free tier covers your database) |
| OpenRouter (AI usage) | Pay-as-you-go, on your own key — typically **$1–2/month** at personal-use volume, for embeddings and enrichment |

There's no subscription to us, and no markup on AI usage — you pay
OpenRouter directly, at their price.

## If your AI credits run out

If your OpenRouter balance hits zero, Thoughtstead doesn't silently stop
working and it doesn't lose anything. You'll see a loud banner on every AI
surface in the app telling you enrichment and semantic search are degraded.
Every capture still saves immediately, raw, first — nothing is ever lost
waiting on AI. The moment you top up your credits, the backlog enriches
automatically.

## Optional upgrades

Neither of these is required — Hobby/free tier is genuinely enough for
personal use:

- **Vercel Pro** — lets you tighten your cron schedule (see
  [Self-deploy](/docs/self-deploy)) so connector sync, enrichment, and
  agents run more often than once a day.
- **Paid Supabase tier** — a bigger database and point-in-time recovery
  (PITR) backups, if you outgrow the free tier's storage or want stronger
  backup guarantees.
