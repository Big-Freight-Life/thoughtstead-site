# Gmail & Calendar

Thoughtstead syncs your Gmail and Google Calendar so messages and meetings show
up alongside everything else you capture.

## Connecting

1. Go to **Settings → Connectors**.
2. Click **Connect** next to Gmail, Google Calendar, or both.
3. Google asks which account you want to use and what Thoughtstead may see.
   Approve it, and you land back in the app connected.

That is the whole setup. There is no Google Cloud project to create, no OAuth
client to register, and no environment variable to set — Thoughtstead is a
hosted service and that plumbing is ours to run.

## What it asks for, and why

- **Gmail** — read access, so your mail can be captured and searched, and send
  access, so a draft you have approved can actually go out. Nothing is ever
  sent without your explicit approval; see
  [Agents & approval](/docs/agents).
- **Google Calendar** — read access, so meetings and their attendees line up
  with what you capture.

Disconnect at any time from the same page. Thoughtstead discards the tokens
Google issued when you do.

## Which context it syncs into

Mail and calendar sync into the context you connected them from. Connect from
Business and your work mail stays in Business. If you want your personal
calendar in your Personal context, switch contexts first and connect it there —
the two never merge.

## Keeping it current

Sync runs automatically once a day. For an immediate pull, use **Sync now** on
the Connectors page.
