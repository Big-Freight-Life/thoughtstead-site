## Updates

Your purchase includes **12 months of updates**. When a new release ships,
you'll see a banner in your app. To update:

1. Pull the latest changes (or, if you deployed via the template flow,
   re-create your repo from the updated template).
2. Redeploy.
3. That's it — database migrations apply automatically during the build on
   Vercel deploys. Self-managed hosts run `npx supabase migration up`
   after pulling.

After 12 months, you keep everything you have — the code, your repo, your
live deployment — forever. You just stop receiving new updates unless you
renew, and future major versions may be offered as separate paid upgrades
rather than free updates.
