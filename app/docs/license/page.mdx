# Plan & billing

Thoughtstead is a hosted subscription: **$20 per month**, billed in advance,
covering one individual account. This page is the plain-English summary — the
[Terms](/terms) are the actual agreement.

## What the subscription covers

- The whole product — capture, semantic search, chat over your brain, people
  and decisions memory, Gmail and Calendar sync, the MCP server, imports, and
  the agent queue.
- **AI usage.** No API key to obtain, no per-token bill, no markup.
- Hosting, backups, and updates. New releases reach you as they ship; there is
  no version to upgrade and nothing to redeploy.
- Support by email at **support@bfl.design**.

## Cancelling

Cancel whenever you like. Your subscription runs to the end of the period you
have already paid for and then stops; you are not billed again.

**Export first — or after.** Your full export stays available while you cancel,
and it includes everything: thoughts, contacts, decisions, and documents, in
formats you can actually read. We keep it free precisely so that leaving is
cheap.

## Price changes

If the price ever changes, we will tell you before it affects you. You can
cancel instead of accepting it.

## Teams

Individual accounts are what ship first. Shared workspaces with roles and seats
come later. If you need that now, email **support@bfl.design** and tell us what
you need — it genuinely helps us prioritise.
