# Plan & billing

Thoughtstead is a hosted subscription: **{HOSTED_PRICE} per {HOSTED_PERIOD}**, billed in advance,
covering one individual account. This page is the plain-English summary — the
[Terms](/terms) are the actual agreement.

## What the subscription covers

- The whole product — capture on desktop, web, and phone; semantic search and
  chat over everything; the design studio and delivery desk; projects, clients,
  contracts, and proposals; meetings, people, decisions, and documents; Gmail and
  Calendar sync; connecting your own AI; imports; and the agent queue.
- **Every context you need** — a second company, a side project, a household.
  Contexts are not seats and are not billed separately.
- **Search and enrichment.** These run on our servers, need no API key, carry no
  per-token bill, and have no markup.
- **Your own AI for agent work.** The delivery desk and the agent queue are driven by the Claude Code or Codex you connect, on your own plan with
  its provider.
- Hosting, backups, and updates. New releases reach you as they ship; there is
  no version to upgrade and nothing to redeploy.
- Support by email at **support@bfl.design**.

## Cancelling

Cancel whenever you like. Your subscription runs to the end of the period you
have already paid for and then stops; you are not billed again.

**Export first — or after.** Your full export stays available while you cancel,
and it includes everything, from every context: thoughts, contacts, decisions,
invoices, and documents, in formats you can actually read. We keep it free precisely so that leaving is
cheap.

## Price changes

If the price ever changes, we will tell you before it affects you. You can
cancel instead of accepting it.

## Teams

Individual accounts are what ship first. Shared workspaces with roles and seats
come later. If you need that now, email **support@bfl.design** and tell us what
you need — it genuinely helps us prioritise.
