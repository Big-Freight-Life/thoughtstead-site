# Start here

Welcome to Thoughtstead. This page is the map — everything else in these docs
hangs off it.

## What arrives after purchase

Three things land in your inbox within a few minutes of checkout:

1. **A GitHub invite email**, inviting you to the private repository at
   `github.com/thoughtstead/thoughtstead`. Accept it there, or from
   [github.com/notifications](https://github.com/notifications) if the email
   is slow to arrive.
2. **A license key on your Polar receipt.** Keep the receipt — the key is your
   proof of purchase if you ever need support.
3. **A zip download of the release**, in case you'd rather grab the source
   directly than clone the repo.

Once you've accepted the invite, you're ready to deploy.

## Choose a path

There are two ways to get Thoughtstead running, and they end up in the exact
same place: your own Vercel project, your own Supabase database, your own AI
key.

- **[Click-and-go](/docs/click-and-go)** — no terminal required. Click a
  button, connect two accounts, paste a couple of values. Best if you're not
  comfortable on the command line.
- **[Self-deploy](/docs/self-deploy)** — you run the commands yourself, in
  your terminal, with full control over local development too. Best if you
  want to run Thoughtstead locally before it's live, or you're already
  comfortable with `git` and `npm`.

Either path lands you in the same place: a first-run **setup wizard** that
checks your database connection, confirms your AI key, and offers to connect
Gmail, Calendar, and your AI tools. From an accepted invite to your first
captured thought takes under 10 minutes.
