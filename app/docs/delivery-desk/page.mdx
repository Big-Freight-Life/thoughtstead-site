# Delivery desk & Coach

The Delivery desk shows what is happening across your clients' AI work and what
needs you. The Coach checks the record as you fill it in and points out what is
missing. The desk is driven by the AI you already use, and the Coach is plain
rules that need no AI. Thoughtstead does not run a model for either one on your
behalf.

## The Delivery desk

One page, client by client. Every client with a project and an engagement
appears on its own, with a short line saying where that engagement stands and
the next thing to do. A client you haven't set up yet shows a **Set up** button;
set it up and Thoughtstead tracks its due dates, overdue upkeep and running
clocks as well. Engagements whose project isn't linked to a client are listed
separately, with a link to attach one.

The block at the top says how many engagements are actually stuck, and it never
says nothing needs you if it couldn't read the engagements.

## The Coach

The Coach looks at what you have written and shows each gap as a gap: what is
missing, and what would close it. It never fills in a value, never edits your
work, and never blocks you from saving, marking a phase done, or exporting.

It checks five things:

- whether a line that needs an owner names a person, not a role or "the team";
- whether a condition could actually fail, rather than "when ready";
- whether a benefit has a baseline, a starting point to measure from;
- whether a condition was written before the result it judges;
- whether a recommendation was written before the numbers were in.

Findings appear in two places. On a phase sheet, a note sits next to the field or
row it applies to, labelled **Coach**, and clears as soon as you fix it. On the
project's consulting overview, one line gives the number of gaps and links to the
first. It only says there are no gaps for a check that actually ran. If a check
couldn't run, it says why. A row you haven't started isn't flagged.

The Coach is rules, so it costs nothing and works whether or not you have
connected an AI.

## Your own AI drives the desk

Connect your own Claude Code or Codex (see [Connect AI tools](/docs/mcp)) and it
can work from the desk through Thoughtstead's tools. It uses your plan with its
provider. Thoughtstead never holds your Claude or Codex login.

What it produces are proposals. A person at the approval gate turns a proposal
into a record, the same rule that applies everywhere else (see
[Agents & approval](/docs/agents)). You choose how much a connection may do, and
you can revoke it in Settings.
