# Delivery desk & Coach

The Delivery desk is where you see what is happening across your clients' AI
work and what needs you. The Coach checks a newcomer's work before anyone relies
on it. Both are driven by the AI you already use. Thoughtstead does not run a
model for them on your behalf.

## The Delivery desk

One page that answers a single question: what changed, what is due, and what is
waiting on a person. It shows proposals awaiting a decision, missing information,
blocked work and overdue upkeep, so recurring work such as retests, re-reads and
reviews is visible and is never cleared just because time passed.

## The Coach

The Coach looks at work a person is about to rely on and shows the gaps. It
checks things like:

- whether every line has a named person, not a role or "the team";
- whether a condition could actually fail, rather than "when ready";
- whether a baseline was captured before a promise was made;
- whether the condition was written before the result it judges.

It is mostly written rules, not a model. It reports what is missing and what
would close the gap. It never fills in a value or edits your work, and it says
"no gap found" only when the check actually ran.

## Your own AI drives it

Connect your own Claude Code or Codex (see [Connect AI tools](/docs/mcp)) and it
can operate the desk through Thoughtstead's tools. It uses your plan with its
provider. Thoughtstead never holds your Claude or Codex login.

What it produces are proposals. A person at the approval gate turns a proposal
into a record, the same rule that applies everywhere else (see
[Agents & approval](/docs/agents)). You choose how much a connection may do, and
you can revoke it in Settings.
